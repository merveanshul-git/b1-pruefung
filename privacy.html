Privacy Policy

B1 Prüfung: Deutsch lernen · Last updated 11 October 2026

B1 Prüfung ("the app") is made by Anshul Jindal. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app. In short: we do not collect any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your study plan, flashcard progress, exercise results, writing drafts and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Speaking-practice recordings are kept temporarily on your device so you can listen back, and are deleted when you leave the screen.

Optional iCloud backup with Sign in with Apple

If you choose to sign in with Apple, the app saves a copy of your study progress in your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). This data belongs to your Apple account; the developer cannot see or access it. Apple shares only an anonymous user identifier and, the first time, the name you choose to share; these stay on your device and in your private iCloud. You can sign out at any time in the app, and revoke access in iPhone Settings › Apple ID › Sign in with Apple.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you tap "Record yourself" in speaking practice. Recordings never leave your device.

No tracking, ads or analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not share data with third parties. Text-to-speech uses the voices built into iOS on your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

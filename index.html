Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Everything works offline. Signing in with Apple is optional and only backs up your progress to your private iCloud.

How do I move my progress to a new iPhone?
On the old iPhone, open the Me tab and sign in with Apple. On the new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple ID — your progress is restored automatically.

The listening audio sounds robotic.
Install better German voices: Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › German, and download an "Enhanced" voice.

Can I change my exam date?
Yes: Me tab › gear icon › Plan.

Is this an official telc app?
No. The practice material is original and follows the exam format; the app is not affiliated with telc gGmbH or the Goethe-Institut.
